What do these numbers mean?
Win rate
— wins ÷ (wins + losses); pushes excluded.
P/L
— units gained/lost assuming a flat −110 price.
CLV*
— mean edge (model − market, in percentage points) at posting time. Sustained > 0 = beating closing line.
Brier
— mean squared error of model probabilities. Lower is better.
Log-loss
— logarithmic scoring rule. Lower is better.
Tier
— premium ≥ 70% · strong ≥ 50% · edge ≥ 30% · lean ≥ 15%.
Baseline total
— when no market O/U line is posted, the model uses the season's league-average total instead.
