Matchups

Per-hitter prop scores for today's slate. Each score is 0–10 anchored at 5.0 = league-average matchup — the pitcher you're facing is by far the biggest factor.

What do these scores mean?
HIT
— probability of 1+ hit. 5.0 = avg, 7+ = elevated.
HR
— home-run likelihood. Needs Statcast (degrades gracefully without).
TB
— total bases (over 1.5). Blend of hit + power.
K
— strikeout (1+). High = K likely; low = batter avoids K.
Switch hitters
— resolved to the opposite hand of today's SP. Shown as S→L or S→R.
